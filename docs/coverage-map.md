# Coverage map

The site owner confirmed online service in all Canadian provinces and territories and all U.S. states on 2026-10-14. Product availability remains conditional. No office addresses or in-person service are implied.

`assets/canada-us-coverage.svg` uses Natural Earth 1:50m admin-1 state/province geometry, downloaded 2026-10-14 from https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_1_states_provinces.geojson. Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

Canada and the U.S. are projected together with a conic conformal projection (standard parallels 30 and 60 degrees, central meridian -100 degrees). Alaska stays in geographic position. The main Hawaiian islands use a separately fitted Mercator inset, explicitly labeled as a different scale. SVG path coordinates are rounded to one decimal pixel. All 13 Canadian regions, 50 U.S. states and Washington, D.C. are retained. The HTML text lists use `_data/coverage.json`.

The SVG is static, contains no remote scripts or tracking, and does not require a map provider key. Do not treat this illustration as an official boundary survey.
